Customer Churn Analysis

Solo project

End-to-end churn study on 3,000 synthetic customers. Cleaned missing data, engineered features, answered business questions with SQL, then compared Logistic Regression and Random Forest pipelines with 5-fold cross-validation and a held-out test set. Finding: basic-plan customers churn the most (18.5% vs ~12.5% on other plans).
